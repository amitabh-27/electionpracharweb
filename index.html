Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters. This starter policy is structured for a mobile survey platform and should be updated with your actual legal entity, contact details, retention periods, processors, and data practices before publication.

Last updated: September 26, 2026

1. Information We Collect

ElectionPracharApp may collect information needed to provide account access and app functionality, such as a phone number for OTP verification, account details, volunteer information, survey responses, booth or campaign information, and technical information required for security and service operation.

2. How We Use Information

Information may be used to authenticate users, provide survey and campaign-management features, maintain volunteer and booth records, improve application reliability, prevent unauthorized access, and provide support.

3. Authentication

Where OTP verification is used, a phone number may be processed to send and validate a one-time verification code. OTPs should not be stored longer than reasonably necessary for authentication and security purposes.

4. Survey and Campaign Data

Survey responses and campaign-management information are processed to provide the features configured by authorized users. Access should be restricted according to account roles and permissions.

5. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

6. Data Sharing

Personal information should not be sold. Information may be processed by service providers that support hosting, authentication, analytics, notifications, or infrastructure, subject to appropriate contractual and security measures.

7. Data Retention

Information should be retained only for as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy, legal requirements, dispute resolution, security, or legitimate operational needs.

8. Your Choices and Requests

Depending on applicable law and the account relationship, users may request access, correction, deletion, or other actions concerning their personal information. Requests can be submitted through the official ElectionPracharApp support contact.

9. Children's Privacy

ElectionPracharApp is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children where prohibited by applicable law. If you believe such information has been collected, contact the service operator so it can be reviewed and handled appropriately.

10. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when the service, legal requirements, or data practices change. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

11. Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, replace this text with your official privacy email, legal entity name, and postal/business contact information before publishing.
